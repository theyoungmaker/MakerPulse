Set up your MakerPulse "Cheap Yellow Display" desk companion straight from this page — no Arduino IDE, no cables to a laptop full of toolchains, just a browser.

This flashing tool uses WebSerial, which only works in Chrome or Edge on desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) — not Firefox, Safari, or any mobile browser.
1

Plug it in

Connect your MakerPulse to this computer with a USB cable.

2

Connect and flash

Click the button below, pick the device's serial port, and wait for flashing to finish.

3

Join your WiFi

A WiFi setup dialog appears automatically right after flashing — no re-flash needed if you ever switch networks.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Please use Chrome or Edge on desktop. This site must be served over HTTPS to flash devices.

Already flashed and just need to reconnect to a new WiFi network? Click the button above again — it detects your device and offers WiFi setup directly, no re-flash needed.
